My learning repository for working through tensors, data loaders, neural networks, autograd, and optimization.

In the first module, I adapted the PyTorch Quickstart image-classification example from FashionMNIST to **FGVC Aircraft**, moving from 10 to 100 classes and handling images with different dimensions.

- Worked through image preprocessing, training loops, loss calculation, and parameter updates.
- Explored additional training epochs, Adam, and shuffled training batches with a simple multilayer perceptron.
- Tracked test loss and accuracy across five epochs, documenting modest improvement and the model's remaining limitations on a 100-class task.
